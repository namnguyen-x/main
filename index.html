Dr. Ta Nam Nguyen

Postdoctoral Researcher – Electrochemistry, Energy Storage & Conversion

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Chungnam National University, South Korea, working on electrochemical catalysis, hydrogen and ammonia electrolysis, and energy conversion materials.

My expertise covers catalyst synthesis and electrodeposition, preparation of catalyst inks and membrane–electrode assemblies, and electrochemical testing (CV, chronoamperometry, EIS) in three-electrode and electrolyzer configurations.

I am also interested in applying electrochemical concepts to practical devices, educational content, and emerging energy technologies.
