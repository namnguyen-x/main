Dr. Ta Nam Nguyen

Postdoctoral Researcher – Energy Science and Technology, Chungnam National University

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Energy Science and Technology with specialized expertise in catalyst design and fabrication for hydrogen production from water and ammonia using electrochemical methods.

My work focuses on developing nanomaterials and electrocatalysts, optimizing membrane–electrode assemblies and porous transport layers, and improving device performance and durability for polymer electrolyte membrane water electrolysis and related systems.

I am passionate about experimental research, precise data analysis, and translating fundamental electrochemistry into practical energy technologies.
